# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The ModernOPT project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to evidence and correction.
- Provide useful context when reporting issues; avoid insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Respect maintainers' time. No one is required to respond immediately or accept every feature request.
- Follow GitHub's terms and applicable law.
- Respect intellectual property, software licences, privacy, and attribution requirements.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, stalking, threats, sexualised abuse, sustained personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Spam and knowingly malicious disruption of project discussions are also prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and the safety of participants.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options available on the maintainer's GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Do not publish someone's private information in an issue.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably possible. Please include links and a concise description, and avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive data.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces where a person is representing the project.
